These pages say what devvm protects you from and what's still yours to watch. [Attack vectors](attack-vectors.md#staying-safe) opens with the habits that close most of what's left.

- [Attack vectors](attack-vectors.md): how to stay safe, and what devvm does about supply chain attacks, rogue agents, git hooks and data theft.
- [Network policy](network.md): the proxy that limits a VM to an allowlist and keeps tokens on the host.
- [Working on a clone](clone.md): `--clone`, a VM with its own clone of the repo instead of the folder mounted.
- [.git protection](git-protection.md): how devvm keeps a VM from changing what git runs on the host.
- [Security model](security.md): what a VM can and can't reach on your machine, and how devvm enforces it.
